---
title: 'Salmon and Potatoes'
description: 'Pan seared salmon with potatoes'
time: '25 min'
servings: 2
price: '$$$'
date: 2025-11-17
cover: '../../assets/placeholder-3.jpg'
ingredients:
  - '2 salmon fillets'
  - '800g potatoes'
  - 'Avocado oil'
  - '1 tablespoon butter'
  - 'Salt and pepper'
instructions:
  - 'Slice potatoes in half and boil until fork-tender, about 15 minutes.'
  - 'Pat salmon dry. Salt both sides.'
  - 'Heat pan with avocado oil on medium-low.'
  - 'Place salmon skin side down and cook 4 minutes.'
  - 'Flip and cook 2 more minutes with butter.'
  - 'Sauté potatoes in avocado oil on medium-high until golden, about 10 minutes.'
  - 'Season potatoes with salt and pepper.'
  - 'Serve together.'
---
