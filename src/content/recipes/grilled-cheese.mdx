---
title: 'Grilled Cheese'
description: 'Crispy grilled cheese'
time: '8 min'
servings: 1
price: '$'
date: 2025-11-16
cover: '../../assets/placeholder-4.jpg'
ingredients:
  - '2 slices bread'
  - '2 slices cheese'
  - '1 tablespoon butter'
instructions:
  - 'Butter one side of each bread slice.'
  - 'Place one slice butter-side down in a cold pan.'
  - 'Add cheese on top.'
  - 'Place second slice butter-side up on cheese.'
  - 'Turn heat to medium and cook 3 minutes until golden.'
  - 'Flip and cook 3 more minutes.'
  - 'Serve hot.'
---
